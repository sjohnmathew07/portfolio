Case Study 01 / Embedded Systems & IoT
Hardware & Web Bridge

Smart Switch Board — Intelligent Electrical Automation System

An electronic switching ecosystem that liberates physical household infrastructure from static wall switches, allowing lights, cooling systems, and electrical appliances to be actuated automatically or remotely with low-latency bidirectional wireless communication.

The Architectural Challenge

Conventional residential switches offer zero remote telemetry, scheduled automation, or assistive control. The objective was to design a fault-tolerant switching system where physical switches, mobile app signals, and sensor triggers work harmoniously without race conditions or electrical safety compromises.

The Engineering Solution

I programmed Arduino and ESP32 microcontrollers to listen for wireless signals over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, as well as polling local sensor states. The microcontroller processes incoming commands and drives multichannel relay modules, electrically isolating digital logic from high-voltage household circuits. In parallel, I architected a web dashboard and mobile interface enabling users to toggle appliances, inspect live state logs, and trigger timed routines.

Case Study 02 / Web Platforms & Infrastructure
Enterprise Dashboard Architecture

Optmyzr — Shareable Live Dashboards & Custom White-Label Platform

A secure client-facing analytics portal enabling digital marketing teams and enterprise agencies to provide external clients with real-time, tamper-proof reporting dashboards under fully personalized corporate branding and custom white-labeled domains.

The Architectural Challenge

High-growth agencies need to share comprehensive campaign performance reports without granting clients administrative access to core operational platforms. Furthermore, agencies require custom branding and custom domains (e.g., reports.agencyname.com) to maintain client trust and enterprise brand cohesion.

The Engineering Solution

Engineered shareable dashboard URLs configured with granular viewing scopes and dynamic client branding injection. Implemented the custom domain routing architecture utilizing DNS CNAME records and automated SSL certificate validation, guaranteeing secure transport over HTTPS. Seamlessly integrated analytical layers across Tableau and SAP data stores to ensure live, synchronized query execution.
